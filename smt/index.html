The Nightmare Begins

Resident Evil is the pioneer of the survival horror genre, originally released in 1996 for the PlayStation. The story follows the S.T.A.R.S. Alpha team as they investigate a series of cannibalistic murders in the Arklay Mountains. It remains a masterpiece of atmospheric tension and resource management.

Survival Horror Facts

The development and history of the Spencer Mansion incident are filled with interesting trivia. Here are six facts about the game that defined a genre:

  • It was originally intended to be a first-person remake of 'Sweet Home'.
  • The game is known as 'Biohazard' in Japan.
  • It holds a world record for the 'Worst Game Dialogue' ever recorded.
  • The Spencer Mansion was inspired by the Overlook Hotel from 'The Shining'.
  • The term 'Survival Horror' was coined specifically for this game's marketing.
  • The 'Zombie Turning' cutscene was the first FMV many players ever saw.
